# P6-F Templates Strategy

## Objective

Define the minimum WhatsApp template strategy for future Respirarte usage without blocking the first controlled real sending test.

## Current decision

Templates are not required for the first controlled real test if the user writes first and Elvira replies inside the 24-hour customer service window.

Templates will be needed later for business-initiated conversations, reminders, follow-ups or messages outside the 24-hour window.

## Template scope for later

Minimum future templates:

- appointment_reminder
- appointment_confirmation_by_staff
- followup_after_therapy
- reactivation_message

Important boundary:

`appointment_confirmation_by_staff` refers only to a future human-approved confirmation message after the Dra. D'Aleman or an authorized Respirarte team member has reviewed the request. It does not mean that Elvira confirms appointments autonomously.

## Current P6-F rule

- Do not create templates yet.
- Do not depend on templates for first controlled activation.
- Keep first real test user-initiated.
- Validate natural conversation first.
- Add templates only after controlled sending is proven stable.

## Notes

Template wording must be short, clear, respectful and aligned with Colombian Spanish using usted.
